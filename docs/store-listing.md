## Detailed description

apted Tracker keeps your job search organized without the spreadsheet.

**Automatic application logging.** When you submit an application on a
supported job board — Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, or LinkedIn — apted
logs it for you: company, role, and the posting you applied from. An undo
button appears right after each capture, so you're always in control.

**A live pipeline, everywhere you work.** A side panel and an on-page drawer
show your applications grouped by stage — Applied, Interviewing (with round
badges), Offer, and a collapsed Closed section. Update an outcome with one
tap; it syncs instantly to your apted account.

**Email awareness (opt-in behaviors, always one-tap).** When a job-related
email arrives in Gmail — an interview invitation, an offer, or a rejection —
apted shows a suggestion card. Nothing changes without your approval: every
suggestion is Apply-or-Dismiss. You can turn passive tracking off entirely in
settings.

**Coming soon: resume tailoring.** Tailored resumes matched to each posting,
built on the same apted account.

**Works offline.** Captures made without a connection queue up and sync
automatically when you're back online.

Notes:
- Requires a free apted account (sign in with Google, GitHub, or email).
- Supported boards: greenhouse.io, lever.co, myworkdayjobs.com,
  jobs.ashbyhq.com, linkedin.com/jobs.

## Data-use disclosures (dashboard form)

- **Website content** — "This extension will read job-posting content
  (company, role, job description) on supported job boards, and the sender,
  subject, and body of job-related emails in Gmail, to log your applications
  and suggest status updates. Content is synced only to your own apted
  account and is never sold or shared." Browsing history on supported sites
  is the declared activity; there are no `tabs`/`webRequest`/
  `webNavigation` permissions and no browsing-history collection beyond
  the pages where the extension runs.
- **User activity** — job applications you submit (company, role, posting
  URL, submission timestamp) are recorded to your account. This can be
  disabled ("Log applications submitted without apted open" in settings).

## Permission justifications

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `activeTab` + `scripting` | Scrape the posting you're looking at when you invoke apted |
| `storage` | Store your connection token, settings, offline queue, and pipeline cache |
| `sidePanel` | The pipeline side panel |
| `host_permissions` (job boards + mail.google.com) | Content scripts that read postings and classify job emails on exactly those origins — never all sites |
| `optional_host_permissions: <all_urls>` | Requested only if you point the extension at a self-hosted apted server |

## Open questions resolved during implementation (sync to Linear PRE-88)

- **Passive tracking default: ON** (core value prop; prominently disclosed
  in the privacy policy and listing; one-checkbox opt-out in settings).
- **Event-first API: standalone `/api/applications/event` route** (main
  POST stays strict) + `tracked_obj` column with a partial unique index for
  server-side dedupe.
- **No `tabs` fallback channel** — content scripts + confirmation
  heuristics cover the supported sites; `tabs`/`webRequest`/`webNavigation`
  would hurt the review story.
- **Gmail ambiguous emails**: local classifier maps the four outcome
  patterns; ambiguous job-related emails use the existing `/api/extract`
  LLM route (company/role only — the route doesn't return a type) and
  suggest as applied-confirm. Non-job emails are ignored entirely.
